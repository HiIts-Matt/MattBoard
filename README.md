> Your space is sacred. A billboard doesn’t belong there.

MattBoard is a free, open source smart display for your wall. Calendar, weather, clock, and more — built with a drag-and-drop editor, running entirely on your own hardware.

No subscription. No account. No watermark in your kitchen.
